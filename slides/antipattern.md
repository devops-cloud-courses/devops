## DevOps Anti-patterns

----

### Dev and Ops Silos

* No communication between Dev and Ops
* The infamous Wall of Confusion
* Lack of operational context

<img src="img/anti-type-a.svg" alt="Dev and Ops Silos topology" />

----

### DevOps Team Silo

* Creates a brand-new silo
* Typically mandated top-down by an executive
* Lack of collaboration with this new silo

<img src="img/anti-type-b.svg" alt="DevOps Team Silo topology" />

----

### No-Ops (or Devs Don't Need Ops)

* Lack of production knowledge on the Dev side
* Assumes the cloud solves every problem

<img src="img/anti-type-c.svg" alt="No-Ops topology" />

----

### DevOps as a Tools Team

* New team on the Dev side working on tooling
* Still no communication or collaboration between Dev and Ops
* Limited impact despite improved tooling

<img src="img/anti-type-d.svg" alt="DevOps as Tools Team topology" />

----

### Rebranded SysAdmin

* Merely a job title change
* No organizational or cultural change
* No change to internal processes

<img src="img/anti-type-e.svg" alt="Rebranded SysAdmin topology" />

----

### Ops Embedded in the Dev Team

* No dedicated Ops team
* The Dev team lacks awareness of production constraints
* Production operations are largely underestimated

<img src="img/anti-type-f.svg" alt="Ops Embedded in Dev Team topology" />

----

### Dev and DBA Silos

* DBAs become a bottleneck for frequent code deployments
* Not included early in the development lifecycle
* Leads to recurring data and database issues

<img src="img/anti-type-g.svg" alt="Dev and DBA Silos topology" />
