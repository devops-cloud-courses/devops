### Other sub-domains may be added in the future

* **Continuous Feedback**: Close the loop with Real User Monitoring (`RUM`), product telemetry, and feature usage analytics
* **DevSecOps**: Shift security left across source code, dependencies (`SBOM`), containers (`Cosign`), and runtime policies (`OPA` / `Kyverno`)
* **Platform Engineering (`IDP`)**: Build self-service Internal Developer Platforms and *"Golden Paths"* (`Backstage`, `Crossplane`, `Kratix`) to reduce developer cognitive load
* **FinOps & GreenOps**: Embed cloud unit economics (`OpenCost`) and carbon/energy efficiency (`Kepler`) into engineering decisions
* **MLOps & LLMOps / AI Agents**: Automate model training, evaluation, deployment (`MLflow`, `Kubeflow`, `Ray`, `vLLM`), and AI-assisted operations
